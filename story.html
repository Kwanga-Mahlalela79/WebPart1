About Us

This Non-Profit organization focuses on providing women and girls all care packages for those who are not fortunate enough to purchase. Feminine hygiene products are becoming more out of reach as they become too expensive, big commercial sanitary companies putting dangerous chemicals and some not privileged enough to be taught how to use take care of their bodies. That is where we come in. We provide personal visits, give packages out, create valuable lessons and share stories to those want to be heard.
