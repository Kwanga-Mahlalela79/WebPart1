# WebPart1 — SaniCare Org Website

Consists of 6 pages: HOME, ABOUT, CONTACT, ENQUIRIES, POLICY, BLOG. It is a Non-profit Organization with the sole purpose of telling stories, open for donations and caring for people who need Feminine hygiene products.
This is a three-part project (HTML, CSS, and JavaScript). Part 1 delivered the homepage structure like the skeleton of the webpages.

## Pages

- **HOME** – Introduces SaniCare Org, our services, payment methods, and email sign-up.
- **ABOUT** –Our history, mission and vision, who we serve, and a "Find us" location map.
- **CONTACT** – Contact details and a message form.
- **ENQUIRIES** – Categorised enquiries: requesting a care package, reporting a situation, volunteering, or a general question.
- **POLICY** – Our privacy, donation, story confidentiality, and care package eligibility policies.
- **BLOG** – Real-life stories and organisation updates from our outreach visits.

## What's new in Part 2

- Part 2 builds out and styles all six remaining pages, and deploys the site live for viewing and navigating.
- Extended `style.css` with different sections for each page, keeping the original colour palette (plum, rose, cream) and typography (Fraunces & Work Sans) consistent throughout.
- Connected the navigation menu across all six pages so it moves correctly between them including the login/sign-up landing page through the 'Stories Told in the Home page.
- Loads of animation through each page such as the menu bar, buttons, open text boxes, and blocks. 
- Added a "Find us" section with an embedded map on the About page.
- Deployed the site live using GitHub Pages.

## Navigation

The logo links back to the homepage, with tabs for each subcategory serving a different purpose within the website. The homepage introduction leads into a call-to-action button, a brief overview of the services we provide, the payment methods available for donating, and an open text box for visitors to enter their email address if they're interested in updates. This is followed by a short 'About Us' summary in the footer.

## Images

Photography and icons create a visual design that is pleasing to the eye and catches the viewer's attention, reinforcing the emotional tone of the organisation's mission.
