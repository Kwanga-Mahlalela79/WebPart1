About Us

This non-profit organization focuses on providing women and girls with care packages for those who are not fortunate enough to purchase them. Feminine hygiene products are becoming increasingly expensive. We provide personal visits, distribute care packages, create valuable lessons and share stories from people who deserve to be heard.
