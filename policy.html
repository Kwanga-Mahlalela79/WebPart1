POLICIES

How we handle your information, your stories, and your donations.

Transparency matters to us as much as the work itself. Here's what you can expect from SaniCare Org.

Last updated: September 2026

Privacy & data

We only collect the information we genuinely need — your name, contact details, and the content of your message or enquiry. We never sell or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

  • Contact form and enquiry submissions are stored securely and only accessed by our team.
  • Email subscribers can unsubscribe from updates at any time.
  • We retain records only as long as needed to support our programs and reporting.

Story & identity confidentiality

Real-life stories shared on this site include details like location, date and the support provided, so our community can see the impact of every donation. We keep the identities of the people involved anonymous by default.

  • A story is only published with a name, photo or identifying detail if the person involved gives explicit permission.
  • You can request that a story involving you be edited or removed at any time.
  • Enquiries submitted through our forms are treated as confidential unless you tell us otherwise.

Donations

Every donation goes directly toward care packages, outreach visits and hygiene education. We currently accept donations via PayPal, EWallet and Payoneer.

  • Where a donation is linked to a specific story or fundraiser, we post an update once it's been received and allocated.
  • Personal financial details are handled by our payment providers directly — SaniCare Org does not store card or account numbers.
  • Donations are non-refundable once funds have been allocated to a care package or outreach visit.

Care package eligibility

Care packages are prioritised for people assigned female at birth who face financial or logistical barriers to accessing hygiene products, with a focus on underserved communities.

  • Requests can be submitted through our Enquiries page.
  • Availability depends on donations received and outreach schedules for your area.
  • We do not require proof of income, but we do ask honest applicants to consider others in greater need.

Community conduct

Our comment and suggestion features are meant to be a safe space. We remove content that is abusive, discriminatory, or intended to shame anyone for their body or circumstances, and we reserve the right to restrict access for repeated violations.

Questions about this policy

If anything here is unclear, or you'd like to exercise any of the rights above, reach out through our Contact page and we'll get back to you.

About Us

This Non-Profit organization focuses on providing women and girls all care packages for those who are not fortunate enough to purchase. Feminine hygiene products are becoming more out of reach as they become too expensive, big commercial sanitary companies putting dangerous chemicals and some not privileged enough to be taught how to use take care of their bodies. That is where we come in. We provide personal visits, give packages out, create valuable lessons and share stories to those want to be heard.
